# NASA-Space-Apps-Challenge-2025
To create an AI-powered educational story that simplifies complex scientific concepts like space weather into fun, visual storytelling for children — promoting curiosity and awareness about how science shapes our world.


🌾 Digital Story: Space Weather Through a Farmer’s Eyes
📘 Overview

This digital storytelling project was created as part of the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2025. The theme revolves around explaining space weather—the Sun’s dynamic influence on Earth—in a simple, engaging, and educational way for children.

The story is told from a farmer’s point of view, helping young minds understand how solar flares, coronal mass ejections (CMEs), and solar wind affect everyday life. The narrative follows a group of children visiting a farm. While exploring, they notice that some crops are spoiled and curiously ask the farmer why. The farmer gently explains how the Sun’s bursts of energy travel through space, influencing farming equipment, crop health, and even the magical auroras seen in the sky.

Through this simple yet meaningful story, children learn how the Sun connects everything on Earth — from plants to people, and from technology to nature.

🛠️ Tools Used

ChatGPT: For idea generation, dialogue writing, and creating story prompts.

Bing AI: For generating images and animations based on the story scenes.

Canva: Used for compiling visuals and creating the animated story video.

InShot: For video editing, adding background music, voice narration, and final touch-ups.
